Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? Here are some quick answers before we start.

How can I start coaching with you? +

Just click the WhatsApp button or fill out the form below. I'll reply personally, and we'll choose the right plan for you.

Do I need to pay in advance? +

Yes, payment is made before your plan begins. Once confirmed, your personalized plan starts within 24 hours.

How will I receive my workout and diet plan? +

You'll get everything digitally through WhatsApp or Google Drive—easy to access on your phone anytime.

What if I'm a beginner or have never gone to the gym? +

No problem. Every plan starts from your current level. You'll get clear instructions and simple routines to build consistency first.

Can I change my plan later? +

Yes, you can upgrade or switch plans anytime. Just text me and we'll adjust it based on your progress and schedule.

Do you offer nutrition guidance too? +

Yes, all plans include nutrition support—customized based on your college lifestyle, preferences, and budget.

What's the best way to contact you? +

WhatsApp is fastest. You can also reach me through the contact form on the site for coaching or other questions.
